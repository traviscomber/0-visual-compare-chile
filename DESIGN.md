# VIDENTIA Design Contract

## Purpose
VIDENTIA is a premium trademark-intelligence product for Chile. The interface must communicate evidence, traceability, context and control — never dashboard theatre.

## Non-negotiables
- Dark-only experience. No white cards, white canvases, paper-like panels or bright content blocks.
- No banking/fintech visual language: no financial charts, credit-card motifs, money iconography or trading-dashboard composition.
- No invented clients, brands, logos, legal outcomes, owners, registration numbers or percentages.
- No opaque similarity score as the primary explanation. Evidence and source come first.
- No generic cyber-security shields or random logo constellations.
- No section may look like it came from a different template.

## Palette
- Canvas: #060A0F
- Elevated canvas: #091018
- Surface: #0C141D
- Surface strong: #101A24
- Border: rgba(214,235,232,.10)
- Text: #F3F7F6
- Muted text: #95A3AE
- Quiet text: #6F7E89
- Primary accent: #66DDCB
- Accent strong: #2BB9A5
- Warm evidence accent: #C8A66A

## Typography
- Editorial display headings: low-to-medium weight, tight tracking, high contrast.
- Body copy: minimum 16px desktop / 15px mobile, line-height >= 1.55.
- Technical labels: monospace, minimum 10px desktop, never used for primary reading.
- Avoid ultra-small metadata. Nothing important below 11px desktop.

## Layout
- Max content width: 1480px.
- One dominant idea per viewport.
- Section rhythm: statement -> evidence -> explanation -> action.
- Use asymmetric editorial grids, but keep reading order obvious.
- Cards are evidence surfaces, not decoration.

## Product visual language
- Product visuals are built from: case file, official-source record, evidence rail, signal modules, context graph, workflow timeline.
- Every synthetic visual must be labeled `Visual representativo` or equivalent.
- Official-source visuals may mirror public INAPI information architecture but must not fabricate real case data.
- Comparison examples use abstract, non-branded geometry or clearly generic product silhouettes — never copied brands.

## Hero
- The hero must explain VIDENTIA in one glance.
- Product UI is the hero visual; no stock photo required.
- No similarity percentage in the hero.
- Show evidence states such as: Fuente oficial, Denominación, Visual, Ámbito, Precedentes.

## Motion
- Reveal on entry, subtle depth shift, connector-line progression and status pulse only when useful.
- No continuous distracting background motion.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility
- Focus visible on interactive elements.
- Contrast must remain readable on every surface.
- No meaning conveyed by color alone.
- Mobile preserves hierarchy and semantic reading order.

## Release gate
A homepage revision fails if it introduces white/bright content blocks, fake evidence, fake scores, illegible microcopy, generic banking/cybersecurity visuals, or inconsistent component styling.
